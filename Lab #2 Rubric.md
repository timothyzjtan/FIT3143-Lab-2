Special Consideration & Extensions

* From the special consideration procedure, short extensions are not available for group assessments, in-class assessments (including in-class tests/quizzes and presentations), practical assessments (including laboratories), mid-semester tests, or scheduled final assessments. For these assessment types, students must instead apply for special consideration.
* Unless a special consideration extension is approved, the student group/team will generally receive a 0 mark if they fail to attend the in-class session, fail to demonstrate the work, or fail to submit the required files to Moodle.
* For students with special considerations approved by the special consideration team, an alternative form of assessment must be submitted no later than the newly approved deadline. Students are reminded to read and follow the special consideration and alternate assessment sections in the assessment specification.
* If the extended due date falls after Week 14 and you plan to submit on/after Week 14, students are advised to contact the Chief Examiner and/or the Unit Coordinator, as grades may need to be withheld to allow extra time for marking.
* Students who have received special considerations but failed to submit the required files to Moodle by the newly approved deadline for their alternative assessment will be treated as having made no submission (or as having submitted late, if the file eventually arrives after the newly approved due date). For alternative assessments, late penalties will still apply for late submissions.
