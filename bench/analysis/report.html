Lab 2 Prime Search Benchmarks

Trial-division prime search under five execution models, measured on a 10-core host. The algorithm is identical in every implementation, so the differences are parallelism, not arithmetic.

Log-log. A straight line means runtime is a power of n; the parallel implementations sit below serial by a roughly constant factor, which is what a fixed speedup looks like on these axes.
